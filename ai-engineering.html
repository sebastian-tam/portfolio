AI Agentic System

RAG-based AI Agentic Enterprise System

Fully local, privacy-first RAG AI system covering four core domains — deployed for inhouse operations and clients.

"Implemented a RAG-based AI Agentic Enterprise system for pricing research, QA & UAT, DBS and CRM compliance support — deployed for both inhouse operations and clients, with all data kept entirely on-premises."
System Overview & Architecture

The system was built on a fully local stack using AnythingLLM and Ollama to ensure no proprietary business data left the organisation's infrastructure — a critical requirement for DBS compliance and competitive sensitivity. Deployed for both inhouse operations and external clients.

The RAG pipeline ingests, indexes, and retrieves from internal document stores, enabling natural language queries across historically siloed datasets spanning pricing, marketing, QA, and compliance domains.

AnythingLLM Ollama LlamaIndex LangChain LangGraph Gemma 4 Python
Four Core Modules
Market Pricing Research
Automated ingestion and synthesis of competitor pricing data, enabling instant market position queries across all council zones.
Marketing Creatives Management
Indexed library of all marketing assets with semantic search — enabling instant retrieval of relevant creatives by campaign type, region, or channel.
QA & UAT Support
AI-assisted quality assurance and user acceptance testing — accelerating release cycles and reducing manual QA overhead across inhouse and client projects.
Driver CRM & DBS Compliance
RAG over driver compliance documents, DBS records, and CRM data — automating checks and reducing manual compliance review time significantly.
Business Impact
Outcomes
  • Eliminated manual research bottlenecks across four departments — pricing, marketing, QA, and compliance
  • All data remains fully on-premises — zero third-party exposure, meeting DBS compliance requirements
  • Reduced compliance and DBS review cycles from hours to minutes
  • Successfully deployed for both inhouse Zippe operations and external client engagements
  • Built a self-improving internal knowledge base that grows with every ingested document
  • Enabled non-technical staff to query complex datasets via natural language
